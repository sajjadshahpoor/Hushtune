Save it now, play it anywhere.

Hushtune is a web app for iPhone that saves audio and video files to your phone, so you can listen and watch offline in any player — no mobile data needed.

▶ Open Hushtune View source on GitHub

Nothing to install from an app store — it runs in Safari and can be added to your Home Screen. Only save files you have the right to download.

What it does

One simple job: get a media file onto your phone, then step out of the way.

🔗

Paste a link

Paste a link to an audio or video file — or to a page that has one — and Hushtune finds the files it can save.

🎧

Audio or video

Keep the full video, or save just the audio track as an .m4a — converted right on your phone.

⭳

Save anywhere

Save to the Files app, or send the file straight to a player such as VLC from the iOS share sheet.

📱

Works like an app

Add it to your Home Screen and it opens full-screen with its own icon — no App Store, no Mac, no setup.

How to install

Takes about ten seconds on your iPhone.

1
Open it in Safari

Go to sajjadshahpoor.github.io/Hushtune/app on your iPhone.

2
Add to Home Screen

Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

3
Save your first file

Open Hushtune from your Home Screen, paste a link, and tap Save to Files / Open in… when it's ready. Keep the app open while a download is running.

Hushtune is an independent project. Streaming services and protected players aren't supported.
